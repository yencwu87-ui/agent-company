# Agent Company: feature film. Treatment

**Style:** Living Screencast. 1920×1080, 30 fps, about 83 s, with a British English voice-over (Kokoro bf_emma) and burned-in captions.

**Subject:** Agent Company 0.2. Every screen in the film is the real page, driven frame by frame:
- Guild mode in four worlds, plus handheld mode on a phone;
- the trust credits;
- the two anatomy figures (the dispatch line and trust credits).

**Structure:** one journey through six chapters, each with one beat:
1. **Forge:** a Rogue gets the full harness.
2. **Deploy:** a wrong drop is refused, the ring turns green, and the horn writes the orders.
3. **The work:** the dispatch line.
4. **Prove:** proof plus the OpenProof seal earns XP and a level-up.
5. **Trust:** an escaped defect costs credits, and under 15 the cage drops.
6. **Worlds:** neon, sky and star worlds, and the phone.

The end card follows.

**Guide:** the mint pixel Warden hops from the terminal caret to the window bars and on to the end card. He is original.

- No real-game characters, names or logos.
- No PSA material.
- No claim words.
